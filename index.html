DIVISION STANDINGS

Player OVR uses position weights, role strengths and calibrated scaling. Team grades use 24 core depth-chart slots, with premium-position weights. A 99 team requires a 99 starting K and P. First-season placement games use seeded division ranks. Later seasons use final division standings. Standings ties: division record, point differential, points scored, then team order. Seven playoff seeds per conference · Division winners lead seeds 1–4 · Divisional round reseeds.
