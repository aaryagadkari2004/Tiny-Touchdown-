Prototype rules: four 2-minute quarters; clock runs during live plays; downs, first downs, touchdowns, safeties and possession changes. Touchdowns award 6, followed by a 1-point kick or a single 2-point play. Automatic touchbacks; meter-controlled punts with returns and playable, unblocked field goals. Field goals: press Space to lock accuracy, keep holding for power, release to kick. Overcharging reduces accuracy; early release reduces range. Kicker Power sets a hard 30–55 yard range cap (ratings 60–99). Accuracy controls window size and meter speed for both teams. Kicker overall averages Accuracy and Power. Punt power remains separate. Accuracy windows narrow with distance. Conversion plays are untimed; interceptions end the attempt. 40-second pre-snap play clock; offensive delay of game costs five yards (half the distance near your own goal). Three timeouts per team per half. Punts: F signals a fair catch during flight; touchbacks start at the 20, out-of-bounds and downed punts at the dead-ball spot. Punt catches are automatic when reachable. Punt blocks, muffs, penalties, fumbles, overtime and franchise saves are not implemented yet. Temporary speed-only records select the fastest returner; other position ratings come later. A tied game ends tied. Teams switch ends at halftime. Esc pauses (P also works). T calls a timeout before the snap or between plays; Enter resumes it. Offense: WASD moves up/left/down/right; F slides. Jukes are deferred. Defense: WASD moves, Space intercepts, contact tackles. Tackles animate knockback; the ball is spotted at forward progress. Standard coverage protects longer than blitz pressure. The current pixel art is the team-art standard; uniforms can be recolored later.
